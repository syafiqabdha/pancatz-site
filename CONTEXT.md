# Pancatz

A creative + print + tech + AI studio based in Kulai, Johor. Serves businesses across Malaysia and Singapore with brand, systems, and automation — sold standalone or as a full partnership.

## Language

### Disciplines

**Creative**: Brand identity, logo design, UI/UX, web design, digital assets, social kits.
_Avoid_: Creative Hub, Design Hub

**Print**: Physical production — business cards, flyers, banners, signage, packaging, menus.
_Avoid_: Print Production, Creative Print

**Tech**: IT support, hardware repair, networking, WiFi, hosting, web development, SEO, monthly Tech Care.
_Avoid_: IT Services, IT Support (that's the offering, not the discipline)

**AI**: Chatbots, n8n workflow automation, AI image pipelines, lead routing, CRM integration.
_Avoid_: AI & Automation, AI & Auto

**Full Partnership**: All four Disciplines bundled. One point of contact, one standard.

### Sales & Delivery

**Service**: Umbrella term for anything Pancatz sells. A Discipline is the category; a Service is what the client buys.
_Avoid_: Product, Package

**Project**: A one-time, scoped piece of work (e.g., a logo, a repair, a chatbot build). Has a price, deliverables, and revisions.
_Avoid_: Job, Gig, Task

**Retainer**: A monthly recurring engagement containing Projects. Comes in three tiers (Starter 8h, Growth 20h, Full 42h). Includes rollover hours and priority response.
_Avoid_: Subscription, Plan, Maintenance contract

**Enquiry**: A potential client who submits the contact form. Not yet a client.
_Avoid_: Lead, Prospect, Contact

**Client**: A business that has engaged Pancatz. An Enquiry becomes a Client after conversion.
_Avoid_: Customer, Account

**Intake**: The form → n8n webhook → Telegram notification pipeline. Receives Enquiries and surfaces them for follow-up.

### Process

**Stage**: One of three phases every Project passes through: **Reach out**, **Build**, **Launch**.
_Avoid_: Phase, Step, Milestone

### Portfolio

**Portfolio**: Past work showcase. Labeled **Selected Work** to visitors.
_Avoid_: Case Study, Gallery, Recent Work, Showcase
